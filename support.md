# Holopeek Support

Email **[support@holopeek.com](mailto:support@holopeek.com)**. We usually reply within a few days.

To help us, please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and, for a card that was recognised wrongly, which card it was.

## Common questions

**Cancel or change a subscription.** Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone open **Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions**. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

**Refunds.** Apple handles refunds: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.

**Pro on a new phone.** Open the app's **Settings** and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in to the same Apple Account.

**Delete your price alert data.** In the app, **Settings › Delete my alert data** deletes your price alerts, push tokens and anonymous user from our server. Do this before deleting the app.

**Your collection.** It is stored only on your phone. An iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone includes it.
